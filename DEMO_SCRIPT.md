# NyayaMitra — Hackathon Demo Script
## Total: 5 minutes

### 0:00 — The Opening (say this FIRST, before touching laptop)
> "Before I show you the app, I want you to meet someone.
> Meena Devi is from Naini, Prayagraj — 15 minutes from where we're sitting.
> Her husband Ramesh has been in Naini Central Jail for 3 years.
> He hasn't been convicted. He's waiting for his trial.
> She has no money for a lawyer. She doesn't understand what 'bail' means.
> She speaks Hindi and Bhojpuri.
> This is what happens when she opens NyayaMitra."

*[Open browser. Login as meena/meena123.]*

### 0:45 — Dashboard (30 sec)
Show: 1 case, bail application drafted, next hearing in 15 days.

> "This is Meena's dashboard. Her husband's case. 3 years in custody.
> NyayaMitra automatically analyzed the IPC sections — 420 and 468 —
> and determined this case is bail-eligible."

### 1:15 — Case Detail + Hindi Summary (45 sec)
Click on the case. Show the AI-generated Hindi summary.

> "Meena cannot read legal documents. So NyayaMitra translated the
> entire case into plain Hindi — what happened, where the case stands,
> and what she should do next. In her language."

Toggle to English. Show same summary.

> "And for the advocate's reference — English."

### 2:00 — Bail Generator (90 sec — THE MAIN MOMENT)
Click "जमानत आवेदन तैयार करें" (Generate Bail Application).
Show the wizard. Fill: Applicant = "Meena Devi", Relationship = Wife.
Select 3 grounds including "3 years in custody without trial."
Click Generate.

*[Wait 3-4 seconds for Gemini to generate]*

Show the full Hindi bail application.
Pause. Let them read the heading. Let them see it's a real, formatted document.

> "This took 4 minutes.
> A lawyer would charge ₹5,000–₹10,000 just to draft this.
> Meena paid nothing."

Toggle to English.

> "She can give this to any DLSA lawyer tomorrow morning."

### 3:30 — Lawyer Finder (45 sec)
Click Lawyers tab. Show 3 lawyers in Prayagraj.
Show the green DLSA box: "Free legal aid for income below ₹1 lakh."
Click "Call Now" on Adv. Ramesh Kumar Verma.

> "NyayaMitra doesn't just draft the application.
> It connects her to a free, empanelled lawyer in her district."

### 4:15 — Ask Nyaya in Hindi (30 sec)
Go to AskNyaya. Type: "जमानत मिलने के बाद क्या करना होगा?"
Show Gemini responding in Hindi with suggested next actions.

> "She can ask anything. In Hindi. At 2AM when she can't sleep."

### 4:45 — Close (15 sec — say this exactly)
> "40 million cases. 75% of prisoners who haven't been convicted.
> 7 to 14 years of waiting.
> NyayaMitra doesn't solve India's judicial backlog.
> But it gives every Meena Devi a fighting chance.
> And it costs her nothing."

*[Stop. Sit down.]*
